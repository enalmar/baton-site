Support

Most problems are one of the four below. If none of them fit, get in touch and describe what you saw.

The other Mac never appears

Both Macs need the same pairing code, and both need Local Network permission. Check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure Baton is switched on. Both Macs also have to be on the same network; some guest and corporate networks block the discovery Baton relies on.

A peripheral is attached to no Mac

A Magic device that has been released stops listening after a while. Switch it off and on with the slider on its edge, then choose Bring to This Mac from Baton's menu.

"Couldn't connect. It may be held by another Mac"

The other Mac still has the Bluetooth link. Open Baton on that Mac and send the peripheral across from there, or open its Bluetooth settings and disconnect the device.

The peripheral is not in the list

Baton only lists devices already paired with the Mac you are on. Pair it once in System Settings → Bluetooth, on each Mac, and it will appear.

Sending a useful report

Open Console, filter by the subsystem com.eam.baton, reproduce the problem, and copy the lines that appear. Those, plus your macOS version and which Macs are involved, are enough to work out almost anything.
