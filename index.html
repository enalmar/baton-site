Two Macs. One good keyboard.

Apple's Magic peripherals will pair with several Macs, but they only hold one connection at a time. Moving one normally means opening Bluetooth settings on two machines and waiting. Baton turns that into a click in the menu bar.

Release, then take

The Mac you leave drops the Bluetooth bond so it stops grabbing the device back. The Mac you move to pairs and connects on its own.

Nothing gets stranded

Baton checks the other Mac is listening before it lets go. If the handover fails, your peripheral comes straight back.

Stays on your network

Your Macs talk to each other directly over your own network, encrypted with a code only you know. Nothing routes through us.

Setup takes about two minutes

How it works

Pair once, the normal way

In System Settings → Bluetooth, pair each peripheral with each Mac a single time. Baton flips ownership afterwards; it doesn't replace that first pairing.

Enter the same code

Baton generates a twelve-character code. Type it into each of your Macs so they recognise one another. That code is also the encryption key.

Click to hand over

Open the menu bar and pick a Mac to send everything there, or open one peripheral's submenu to move just that one.

The whole interface

Baton has no Dock icon and no window in your way. A checkmark shows what is on this Mac right now. Click a Mac to send everything, or use a peripheral's submenu to move one thing.

Questions

Which peripherals does it work with?

Apple's Magic Keyboard, Magic Mouse and Magic Trackpad from 2015 onwards. Other Bluetooth keyboards and mice often work too, but they are not supported and some refuse to re-pair quickly enough.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to buy it and to activate your key once. After that Baton works entirely on your own network. It rechecks your licence about once a day and keeps working for a month without reaching the internet at all.

How many Macs can I use it on?

Every Mac you personally own. If you replace a machine you can free up its slot from your purchases page and activate the new one.

What happens when the trial ends?

Switching pauses and nothing else changes. Baton never releases a peripheral on its way out, so whatever you were using stays connected to the Mac that has it. Enter a key and switching resumes immediately.

I lost my licence key.

It is in the receipt email you were sent when you bought it. If you cannot find that, get in touch with the email address you paid with and it will be resent.

Is my typing sent anywhere?

No. Baton connects and disconnects Bluetooth devices; it never reads what you type or where you point. Your Macs exchange only device names and Bluetooth addresses, encrypted with your pairing code. See the privacy note.

Why isn't this on the Mac App Store?

Handing a Bluetooth device between machines needs to unpair it, which App Store sandbox rules do not allow. Baton is notarised by Apple and distributed directly instead.
