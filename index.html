Questions

Which peripherals does it work with?

Magic Keyboard, Magic Mouse and Magic Trackpad from 2015 on. Other Bluetooth devices often work, but aren't supported.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to buy and activate. After that it runs on your own network, and keeps working for a month with no connection at all.

How many Macs can I use it on?

Every Mac you own. Replace a machine and you can move its slot across.

What happens when the trial ends?

Switching pauses. Nothing is released, so whatever you were using stays connected. Enter a key and it resumes.

I lost my licence key.

It's in your receipt email. Can't find it? Email us from the address you paid with.

Is my typing sent anywhere?

No. Baton connects and disconnects Bluetooth devices. It never reads what you type. See the privacy note.

Why isn't this on the Mac App Store?

Unpairing a device isn't allowed under App Store sandbox rules. So it's notarised by Apple and sold directly.
