You've got two Macs and one good keyboard.

A Magic Keyboard will happily pair with both, but it only talks to one at a time. So every switch means Bluetooth settings on this Mac, then Bluetooth settings on that Mac, then waiting.

Baton does that bit for you. The Mac you're leaving lets go, the Mac you're going to picks it up. If anything goes wrong, the keyboard comes back to where it was.

Good to know

  • Works with any number of Macs. Two, three, the one in the office. They all show up in the menu.
  • Move one thing or everything. Send the trackpad to the laptop and keep the keyboard where it is.
  • Handles sleep. A sleeping Mac lets go of its devices and takes them back when it wakes.
  • Nothing leaves your network. Your Macs talk to each other directly. There's no account and no server in the middle.
  • Notarised by Apple. Sandboxed, and asks only for what's below.

What it asks for, and why

  • Bluetooth. To connect and disconnect your keyboard, mouse and trackpad. That's the whole job.
  • Local network. To find your other Macs and tell them when a device is on its way.
  • Notifications. Only to tell you if a handover failed. Say no and it just stays quiet.
  • Your keychain. Baton keeps your pairing code and licence key there, the same place Safari keeps passwords. It reads only its own entries, and macOS won't prompt for them unless you've had a different build of Baton on the same Mac before.

Questions

Which devices?

Magic Keyboard, Magic Mouse and Magic Trackpad, 2015 or newer. Other Bluetooth keyboards and mice often work but aren't supported.

Does it need the internet?

Only to activate your key. After that it runs on your own network and keeps working for a month with no connection at all.

What if the handover fails?

Baton checks the other Mac is listening before it lets go. If it still fails, the device comes back to the Mac it was on.

I lost my key.

It's in your receipt email from Polar. Can't find it? Email me from the address you paid with.

Why not the App Store?

Unpairing a Bluetooth device isn't allowed in App Store apps. So Baton is notarised by Apple and sold here instead.
