Licence help

Your key is a single line of text that arrives by email when you buy. There is no account and no password.

Entering it

Open Baton's menu in the menu bar, choose Settings…, then the Licence tab. Paste the whole key and press Activate. Capital letters, spaces and stray line breaks from the email are all handled for you.

Using it on another Mac

Enter the same key on each Mac you own. Every activation takes one slot, and the slot is labelled with that Mac's name so you can tell them apart.

Replacing a Mac

If you have run out of slots, open your purchases page at Polar, remove the activation belonging to the Mac you no longer use, and activate the new one. In Baton, Free Up a Mac… in the Licence tab takes you straight there.

Lost the key

Search your email for the receipt from Polar. If it is gone, email support from the address you paid with and it will be sent again.

Baton says it needs an internet check

Baton rechecks your licence about once a day and keeps working for a month without reaching the store at all. If it has been longer than that, connect to the internet and press Check Now in the Licence tab. A dropped connection alone will never stop a switch.

Refunds

Within thirty days, for any reason. Ask and it will be refunded; the licence stops working at that point.
